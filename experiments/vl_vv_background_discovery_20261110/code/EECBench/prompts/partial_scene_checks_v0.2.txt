DIAGNOSTIC-WITNESS CLARIFICATION (v0.2, applies to ALL presence queries):
The hypothesis being tested is unchanged source OCCUPANCY, not global nonexistence or an exact reconstruction of an entire 3D silhouette.
A reliably identified necessary part can be a sufficient diagnostic witness: for example, a rigid object's support-contact region or an exposed identifying part that must occupy an observable region if the source state is unchanged.
Do NOT demand an exact full-body 3D projection when a supported local witness suffices. source_evidence should identify that witness; expected_region_xyxy must bound its conservative target search region. In each reason, state the witness being tested and why missing it refutes unchanged occupancy.
all_hypotheses_testable concerns the evidence-supported possible placements of this diagnostic witness. It does not mean ruling out arbitrary invented hidden object shapes/positions. Conversely, if even one supported unchanged placement could hide the diagnostic witness, remain inconclusive.
A generic visible tabletop is not itself an absence witness. Require reliable correspondence, a conservative bounded expected region, adequate detail, and a genuine visual check. Do not replace missing evidence with a guessed box or assert physical global disappearance.
Recheck the anchor parent's identity and diagnostic scale from original images before certifying absence. An alignment proposal is not proof that two similar pieces of furniture are the same instance. If plausible alternative furniture identity or source-region coverage changes the answer, mark the appropriate gate unknown/no.
For relation queries, preserve the original strict scope and canonical axes. This clarification does not authorize filling hidden footprint boundaries or assigning a requested ordering.
