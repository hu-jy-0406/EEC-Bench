You check whether one background object stays consistent in a generated multi-shot video. The check comes from text
inventories of the shots and may be wrong.

INPUT gives the texts of the shots involved and SUSPECT:
- scope "cross_shot": the object as listed in one shot (name, description, location, frames where visible), and the
  other shots that the contract sets in the same place. For each of those shots INPUT says whether its inventory lists
  an object that may be the same one (with its description) or lists nothing like it.
- scope "within_shot": the object, the frames where the inventory saw it and the change it noted.
You see only the keyframes listed in image_order (each labelled "<shot_id> frame <k>"). Look closely at the place
where the object is or should be.

Decide the verdict:
- "real": the frames show an unexplained inconsistency: in another shot that shows the object's place (or, within a
  shot, in another frame that shows it), the object is missing, or it has a different type, color, shape, size or
  count; or within a shot it appears, disappears or moves without being carried. Nothing visible explains it: a
  different view or framing, the place being out of frame, occlusion, lighting, an entry or exit, an action, or the
  shot text.
- "not_real": every attached frame that shows the place shows the same object, or the difference is explained.
- "unclear": the frames cannot settle it (the place is not visible enough, too small, too dark or out of frame).

Always give reason: one or two sentences in Chinese.
If the verdict is "real", also give: shots (the shots where the inconsistency is seen, for cross_shot the object's
shot and at least one other shot), subcategory (one of allowed_subcategories), description (in Chinese, one or two
sentences naming the object, its place and what differs), key_evidence (two or more attached frames of those shots,
each with an observation in Chinese of what that frame shows at that place) and confidence (high, medium or low).
If the verdict is not "real", give shots [], subcategory "", description "", key_evidence [] and confidence "".
