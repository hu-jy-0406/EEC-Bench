You check one suspected visual consistency problem of a background object in a generated multi-shot video. The
suspect comes from text inventories of the shots and may be wrong.

You see only the keyframes listed in image_order (each labelled "<shot_id> frame <k>") and, in INPUT, the texts of the
shots involved and SUSPECT: the object or objects with their inventory descriptions and locations, and the suspected
difference. Look closely at the described places in the attached frames.

Decide the verdict:
- "real": the frames show the difference: the object is at its place in one frame and not at the same place in
  another frame that shows that place, or it has a different type, color, shape, size or count, and nothing visible
  explains it (camera motion or a different view, occlusion, lighting, an entry or exit, an action, or the shot text).
- "not_real": the frames contradict the suspect: the object is present and the same in every frame where its place is
  visible, or the difference is explained.
- "unclear": the frames cannot settle it (the place is not visible enough, too small, too dark or out of frame).

Always give reason: one or two sentences in Chinese.
If the verdict is "real", also write the issue: subcategory (one of allowed_subcategories in INPUT), description (in
Chinese, one or two sentences naming the object, where it is and what differs), key_evidence (two or more attached
frames, each with an observation in Chinese of what that frame shows at that place) and confidence (high, medium or
low). If the verdict is not "real", give subcategory "", description "", key_evidence [] and confidence "".
