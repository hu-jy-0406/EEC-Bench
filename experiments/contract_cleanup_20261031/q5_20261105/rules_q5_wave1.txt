THE FOUR RULINGS
Q5-1 Focus, softness, blur. A statement that part of the image is blurred, soft, softened, out of focus or in focus, or that focus shifts ("blurred palms rise behind her", "against softly blurred sand", "softly focused", "soften the left foreground", "rack focus to the door"), is camera information of that shot: record it in that shot's camera_view, in the prompt's words (for example "Medium view; the palms behind her are blurred."). Keep the words already in camera_view: give set_camera the complete new value and every quote it rests on (the old quotes and the new ones). If the same point is now an appearance requirement or part of a description (for example "The palms appear blurred."), remove it there, so it is stated once. A global statement that applies to every shot goes into every shot's camera_view. "Softly" describing motion or sound ("softly rolling sea", "speaks softly") is not about focus.
Q5-2 "Hold". "Hold the shot / view / two-shot / frame", "the camera holds", "hold on <subject>", "hold this single view" is a static-camera requirement: record it in that shot's camera_movement in the prompt's words (for example "The camera holds the two-shot (static camera)."). Keep any other movement the shot states, in order ("Slow push-in, then the camera holds."). If camera_movement already says the camera holds or is static, change nothing (outcome already_recorded). A person holding an object, a pose or an expression ("holds the wrist", "holding a drawn brow", "the held pose") is NOT this ruling (not_applicable).
Q5-3 Lighting stated in one shot. Decide from context whether the lighting is a lasting property of the place or a state of that moment. A lasting property (the place is lit that way whenever it is seen: "the warmly lit hallway", "a dim bar", "a sunlit beach") belongs in the location's description. A state of that moment (it happens or holds in this shot only: "sunlight flares across the window", "the lamp light dims", "her face is lit by the screen", "neon flickers on") belongs to that shot only, as an appearance requirement of the lit location or entity (add_requirement, dimension appearance). Make the contract state it once, in the right place: add it where it belongs, and remove or edit the wrong place (remove_requirement; edit_description to delete it from a description). Lighting already recorded in the right place needs nothing (already_recorded). Lighting stated in every shot where the place appears, or in the global prompt, is a lasting property. When the context does not show that the lighting is the place's lasting look (for example light or weather stated in only one of several shots of the place: "In soft overcast light" in Shot1 only), record it for that shot only: the narrower requirement. Brightness of a color ("bright blue lining") is not lighting.
Q5-4 Ground. A street, road, snow, sand, grass, pavement, path or similar ground is part of its location, like floors: it is not a separate entity. If the contract has it as a separate entity, merge it into the location entity (merge_entities with "into" = the location, "label": "location", and a merged description that keeps the location's attributes and the ground's). Requirements about the ground then refer to the location; keep them (for example "Snow dusts the courtyard." stays a requirement of that shot). Plants, bushes or objects lying on the ground are not ground.
