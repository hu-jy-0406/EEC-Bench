You make an inventory of the background objects in one shot of a generated video. A later step compares the
inventories of different shots and frames to find consistency problems.

You see the six keyframes of one shot (each image is labelled "<shot_id> frame <k>"; frames are in time order) and, in
INPUT, the shot's prompt text, the contract entities (what the prompt specifies), REGISTERED_SPECIFIED (prompt entities
an earlier detection stage found in this shot) and REGISTERED_UNSPECIFIED (objects the earlier stage found in this
shot that the prompt does not name, each with the keyframes where it was boxed).

Do three things:
1. view: one sentence on which part of which place the shot shows (for example "living-room corner: TV under a wall
   clock, hallway door at right"). If the camera moves to another area during the shot, say so.
2. Keep every object of REGISTERED_UNSPECIFIED: one entry each, with its id as ref. Look at all six frames, not only
   the boxed ones. If it is not visible in any keyframe, give frames_visible [] and say so in description.
3. Add every other object that is clearly visible in at least one keyframe, is not in REGISTERED_UNSPECIFIED or
   REGISTERED_SPECIFIED, and is not named by the prompt or the contract: a prop, a piece of furniture, a plant, a
   vehicle, an animal or a person. Include small and background things: items on walls, shelves, tables, counters and
   windowsills; signs, notices, switches, lamps and decorations; things held in a hand. Also list things that lie on,
   hang on or stand on a registered or prompt-named object (a bedspread or blanket on a bed, cushions on a sofa, items
   on a table, chair or shelf), canopies, awnings and fixtures attached to buildings, and objects at the edges and in
   the far background of the frame, walls, floors, ceilings, the ground, rugs, trees and plants, and the clothing,
   shoes, headwear, glasses, jewelry, belts and bags that people wear or carry (one object per item). Go through the
   frames one by one; small objects matter. Use ref "new".

Do not list: the sky or the place as a whole; body parts, hair or makeup; a part of a listed or registered object;
reflections, shadows or light spots. A large number of same-kind items gathered together (a pile of books, a row of
bottles) is one object; a few separate items are separate objects.

For each entry give:
- ref: the REGISTERED_UNSPECIFIED id, or "new";
- name: 1 to 4 words;
- description: type, color, shape, material, size, and anything that tells it apart from similar objects;
- location: where it is in the frame and next to which things (left/right/top/bottom; on, in front of or beside what);
- frames_visible: every keyframe number in which it is visible;
- change_within_shot: "none" if it looks the same and stays at its place in every frame where it is visible;
  otherwise one sentence on what appears, disappears, moves without being carried or pushed, or changes type, shape,
  color, size or count, with the frame numbers. Camera motion, people passing in front of it, and actions shown in the
  frames are not changes.

Report only what you can see. Write in English.
